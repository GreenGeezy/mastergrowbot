Unsupported client names, metrics, founding dates, location and competitor comparisons are omitted. The supplied X post informs structure only.

There is no resetting timer or claim of a historical $89 sale price.
5. **Measure before adding complexity.** Observe sample opens, checkout opens, checkout starts and completed purchases in Whop. Judge paid traffic by contribution after fees, refunds and acquisition costs. Do not optimize solely for clicks.
