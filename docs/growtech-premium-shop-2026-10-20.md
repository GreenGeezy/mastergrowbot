# GrowTech shop redesign — 20 October 2026

## What changed

The shop now leads with a warm editorial hero and a compact comparison of the actual $59 soil/Quickstart bundle, $119 APEXEL/digital bundle and $289 compact tent/guide. Contents, price, destination, delivery estimate, limitations and checkout appear together. Detailed galleries use disclosure controls instead of repeating full sections. The $19/$9 digital offers, attributed APEXEL reviews and existing checkout paths remain intact. New external-retailer click events are kept separate from GrowTech purchases.

Four product recommendations were researched: Govee H5075 Bluetooth temperature/humidity monitor, Fiskars Micro-Tip snips, Boveda storage humidity packs, and Spider Farmer SF1000 100W dimmable light. They are clearly marked as external retailer purchases, not GrowTech inventory, not bundled accessories and not commission-producing links. No unqualified payment plans or fulfillment promises were created. The 60×60cm tent footprint accommodates the SF1000's listed 32.5×29cm fixture, but its 140cm height, support-bar load, hanging hardware, ventilation, electrical version and manufacturer clearances still need verification for a complete installation.

The SF1000 manufacturer search listing showed $69.99 USD and 4.98/5 from 43 ratings. These are dated reference figures, not a current delivered quote or review rich-result markup. Listing ratings can span earlier product versions. No unsupported best-seller, conversion, sales-volume, yield or weekly-profit claims appear in the shop.
