## Thirty-day execution and decision rules

Days 1–7: complete the cost/dispatch gate, page/Whop alignment, guide access, mobile journey and attribution checks. Days 8–14: distribute two prepared organic assets weekly through existing accounts, and use the homepage and comparison-article links. Days 15–21: address the largest measured dropout and reconcile the first profitable order. Days 22–30: repeat placements producing qualified visitors. No paid ads, purchased samples, founder sales calls or speculative partnerships.

Fewer than 100 qualified offer visitors by day 14: improve distribution. >=100 visitors but <5 checkout visits: improve offer clarity, compatibility and real evidence. >=10 genuine checkout visits without payment: inspect errors, delivery objections and surprise charges. Only after these issues are fixed and 200 qualified visitors yield no sales may one seven-day $99 test run, if contribution still >=$40. Compare contribution per qualified visitor; restore $119 when the test does not improve it. Pause promotion on dispatch failure or contribution < $40.

Nine $119 hardware orders = $1,071 gross item revenue. At 2% conversion this needs about 450 qualified visitors; at 1%, about 900. These are scenarios, not sales guarantees. Start the 30-day sales clock when paid ordering is actually ready.
