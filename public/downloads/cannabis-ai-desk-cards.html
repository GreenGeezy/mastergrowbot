Three AI desk cards for cannabis operators

Use these with documents you can share. Copy the prompt and attach the source. Each card includes a quick way to check the answer.

Before the vendor demo

Give your assistant a vendor’s one-page proposal.

List the three claims that would most affect our buying decision. For each, quote the claim, identify the evidence supplied, and give me one live demo request that could reveal a failure. Mark missing evidence explicitly.

Check: Pick the most expensive promise. Can the rep reproduce it with an example you chose?

Before you choose the cheaper quote

Attach two equipment or software quotes. Remove contact and payment details first.

Compare the 12-month cost at the same volume. Separate setup, recurring fees, optional extras and cancellation costs. Cite the page beside each number. Leave missing prices blank and tell me which missing item could reverse the decision.

Check: Recalculate one total yourself. Ask the vendor to price the missing item in writing.

Before you forward an earnings headline

Attach a public cannabis company’s quarterly filing.

Find the R&D expense row. Extract this quarter, last year’s quarter, year to date, and last year to date. Include currency, units and page. Calculate each year-over-year change and explain why the two comparisons might tell different stories.

Check: Read the column headings yourself. A three-month number can look convincing beside a nine-month number.
