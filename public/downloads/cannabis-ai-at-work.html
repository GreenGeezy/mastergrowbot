Using the operating expenses table, compare research spending for the three and nine months ended September 30. Give all four figures in their original units. Calculate each year-over-year change.

Turn a product sheet into a staff FAQ

What can a sales rep promise?

Source product: The Master Cannabis IPM Playbook. The product page describes a field manual with scouting logs and printable checklists.

Try the prompt

Use only this product page. Answer three questions for staff: Who is it for? Does it include scouting logs? Does the purchase include a hardware kit? Cite supporting details. Flag anything the page leaves open.

Worked answer. Audience: legal cannabis growers. Scouting logs: included. Hardware: purchased separately from the book.

Editor’s check. If someone asks for today’s price or a particular format, send them to the current listing. The product description cannot confirm either.
