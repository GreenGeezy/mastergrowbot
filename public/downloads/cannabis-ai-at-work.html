Retail & purchasing desk

What runs out before delivery?

Compare the last seven days of unit sales with stock on hand and supplier lead time. Change a number in the table to see which line needs attention first.

Illustrative shop counts. Daily pace = units sold ÷ 7. Shortfall = rounded-up demand through delivery minus stock, floored at zero. Open orders, changing demand and safety stock need a separate check.
